# Athlete Alphabet V4.4.3 — MLB 2026 regular season

This update bundles 2026 batting and pitching appearance exports (736 batting rows and 943 pitching rows). Each player is matched to the existing MLB database and their 2026 teams are added. Players traded during 2026 count for each club they appeared with. MLB records with no 2026 appearance are not counted as 2026 simply because they were on a July roster.

All NHL, NFL, NBA and All Sports data are retained from V4.4.2. Existing saved progress keys are unchanged; do not reset progress.

## Install
Upload and replace all nine files in the root of the GitHub Pages repository, commit to main, then open with ?v=443.

## Data note
Sources are the two user-provided 2026 MLB batting and pitching CSV exports. Name-only matching can conflate namesakes, and batting/pitching exports may omit players absent from both exports. A final identity and source-completeness audit is recommended before public sharing.
